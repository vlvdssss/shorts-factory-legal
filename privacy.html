Privacy Policy - shorts_factory

Last updated: October 7, 2026

1. Overview

shorts_factory ("the App") is a personal desktop tool used by its owner to upload their own videos to their own TikTok account. This policy explains what data the App handles.

2. Data we access

When the owner signs in with TikTok, the App receives, through TikTok Login Kit, an authorization code and then an access token and refresh token for that account, plus basic profile information permitted by the user.info.basic scope (such as display name and avatar). Through the Content Posting API (video.upload scope) the App sends video files chosen by the owner to the owner's TikTok drafts.

3. How data is stored

Access and refresh tokens are stored only in a local file on the owner's own computer. They are not uploaded to any server, database or third party. Video files are processed locally and sent only to TikTok.

4. What we do not do

The App does not collect data about other TikTok users, does not sell or share personal data, does not use analytics, advertising or tracking tools, and does not transfer data to third parties other than TikTok itself when the owner uploads a video.

5. Retention and deletion

Tokens stay on the local computer until the owner deletes the local token file. The owner can also revoke the App's access at any time in TikTok under Settings and privacy, Security and permissions, Apps and services.

6. Children

The App is not intended for use by anyone under 18.

7. Changes

This policy may be updated from time to time. The latest version is always available at this page.
